## Paj

Hi. I go by **Paj**. I live in Sweden.

I am not a developer by trade. I build things anyway, mostly by describing what I want until it exists, and I have opinions about how they should feel long before I know how to make them work.

## What I am building

**TAG** is the big one: an iOS app for playing real-life tag with friends for a whole month. One person is *it*, tags are verified with GPS instead of arguments, and whoever is *it* when the season ends carries the shame until next August. The whole story is in the [devlog](/devlog/).

Everything else here is smaller and made for fun: quizzes about the shows I grew up with, arcade games short enough to play while the kettle boils, and whatever I get curious about next.

## Why this site exists

My friend has [johanslekstuga.com](https://johanslekstuga.com) — a handmade playground of small projects. I wanted my own. Not a portfolio, not a brand. A shelf.

## How it is made

Hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript, generated by a small Python script with no dependencies. No frameworks to update, no build tools to break. The whole site is a folder of files in git.

Say hi in the [guestbook](/guestbook/) if you found something you liked.
